ABOUT THE BUILDER

Curious about how attacks become evidence.

I’m developing toward a career in SOC and security operations, with a particular interest in the point where logs, indicators, investigation and analyst decisions come together.

Instead of treating cybersecurity as a list of technologies to memorize, I prefer practical work: taking a suspicious email apart, generating events in a lab, searching telemetry, writing a detection, or automating repetitive IOC lookups.

My current learning path combines hands-on labs, CTF problem solving, security projects and technical writing. The goal is to keep building the investigation mindset and technical foundations needed for real security operations.
